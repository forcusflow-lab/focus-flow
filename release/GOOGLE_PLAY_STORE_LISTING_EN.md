## Full description

**Finish what matters. Then unlock what distracts you.**

Focus Flow is a calm, local-first planner for people who want their daily intentions to have real follow-through. Create required To-dos and habits, set a routine, and choose the apps you want to keep out of reach until your essential work is done.

Unlike a timer-only app blocker, Focus Flow connects your daily plan to your app boundaries. When your required items are complete, your selected apps become available again.

### Plan your day clearly

Create To-dos with due dates, priorities, repeating schedules, subtasks, and check, count, or minute-based progress goals. Build habits with daily targets and see your current streak and weekly progress.

### Make commitments visible

Mark an item as **Required** when it should count toward unlocking your selected apps. Keep ordinary tasks separate so your gate stays clear and understandable.

### Set routines that fit your life

Create named rules for mornings, work hours, evenings, or any schedule you choose. Pick the days, start time, end time, and apps that each rule applies to.

### Optional Android focus gate

On Android, Focus Flow can use the optional AccessibilityService to detect when a selected app comes to the foreground and apply your chosen focus rule. It does not read screen text, messages, typed content, or screenshots. Focus Flow does not send your task, note, or app activity data off your device. You can turn this feature off anytime in Android settings.

### Keep your thoughts nearby

Capture notes before they become tasks, then convert them into a regular To-do when you are ready. Adjust text size, color theme, and card transparency for your preferred reading density.

### Built for your device

Your tasks, habits, notes, schedules, and display settings stay on your device. No account is required to begin, and you can delete all Focus Flow data from the Privacy & data screen.

### Free plan, Plus, and timed items

The free plan includes up to 2 tasks, 2 habits, 2 notes, and 5 limited apps in total. Focus Flow Plus is an optional subscription that removes these limits and lets you save named appearance sets. Timed tasks and habits complete after their configured time elapses. If you need to finish one early, the app can show a one-time consumable product from your store for that item.

Focus Flow is a self-management tool, not a parental-control, device-management, or security application. The Android focus gate is optional and cannot guarantee uninterrupted restriction under every device, operating-system, or user-configuration condition.

## ASO terms to validate

Use these terms naturally in the listing and test them with store-listing experiments only after policy review: `focus planner`, `habit tracker`, `task blocker`, `digital wellbeing`, `app blocker`, `daily routine`, `productivity planner`.

## Do not claim

Do not describe Focus Flow as an unbreakable lock, parental-control service, monitoring tool, or system-level device-management product. Do not claim that it blocks all apps in all circumstances, prevents all bypasses, or reads no device information. Keep the AccessibilityService disclosure consistent with the in-app explanation and the Data safety form.
